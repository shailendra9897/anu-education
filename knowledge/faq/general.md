# ANU Education — Frequently Asked Questions

Cross-cutting FAQs for counselling and AI assistants.

## How can a student book a free demo?

Ask the student to contact ANU Education through WhatsApp or email. Use +91 94281 86817 for the main desk or +91 7016497087 for course details.

*Source: ANU course fee PDFs*

## Are classes online?

Yes. ANU Education course materials describe live online classes, portal access, video lessons, mock tests, recordings, and expert feedback.

*Source: ANU course fee PDFs*

## Are prices retail prices or partner wallet prices?

The fee PDFs and B2B brochure describe wallet or limited-offer pricing. Confirm final payable retail price, GST, batch availability, and discounts with ANU Education before enrolment.

*Source: B2B partner brochure and fee PDFs*

## Does ANU Education provide study abroad counselling?

Yes. Duolingo materials mention free study abroad counselling, and ANU positions itself as a study-abroad gateway. For country-specific admissions or visa counselling, collect the student profile first.

*Source: DUOLINGO_FEE.pdf and B2B brochure*

## Are recordings available if a student misses class?

Several course PDFs mention recordings for missed lectures. Confirm recording access duration for the selected course pack.

*Source: PTE_FEE.pdf, GRE_FEE.pdf, FRENCH_FEE.pdf, GERMAN_FEE.pdf, DUOLINGO_FEE.pdf*

## What should the AI do when a student asks about visa success guarantees?

Do not promise approval. Explain that visa decisions are made by the relevant government authority and depend on eligibility, documentation, finances, genuineness, and current policy.

*Source: AI operating policy*

# German Language Coaching

## Overview

- **Best for:** Study in Germany, work and migration, Goethe exam preparation, European academic/career mobility
- **Levels:** Basic, A1, A2, B1, B2

## Eligibility

Open to all students. No prerequisite qualifications required for enrolment.

## Course Details

- **Features:** Live 60-90 minute sessions, C1-certified trainers, vocabulary quizzes, PDF reference material, speaking practice with real-time feedback, grammar practice, Goethe reading and listening tests, session recordings
- **Learning hours:** Up to 200+ live hours in Basic-to-B1 pathway
- **Exam focus:** Goethe-aligned curriculum covering A1, A2, B1 and B2 exam patterns

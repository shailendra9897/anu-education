# Shorter GRE

## Overview

- **Best for:** Master's, MBA, PhD, STEM graduate admissions, scholarship positioning

## Eligibility

Open to all students preparing for GRE. No prerequisite qualifications required for enrolment.

## Exam Details

- **Conducting body:** ETS
- **Duration:** 1 hour 58 minutes
- **Format:** Computer-based and section-adaptive
- **Score validity:** 5 years
- **Retake rule:** Once every 21 days
- **Exam fee estimate:** About INR 22,000
- **Sections:**
  - Analytical Writing: 1 Issue essay, 30 minutes, score 0-6
  - Quantitative Reasoning: 2 sections, 27 questions total, 47 minutes, score 130-170
  - Verbal Reasoning: 2 sections, 27 questions total, 41 minutes, score 130-170

## Course Details

- **Features:** 230+ vocabulary videos, 500+ vocabulary quiz questions, 45+ hours verbal videos, 45+ hours quants videos, 10 timed mock tests, simulated GRE adaptive test engine, instant mock-test results

# Australia

## Overview

- **Positioning:** Destination for higher education, vocational education, ELICOS, and packaged courses.

## Eligibility

Requires Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) from an approved provider.

## Recommended Tests

- IELTS Academic
- PTE Academic
- TOEFL where accepted
- Duolingo (for selected admissions only)

## Visa Process

**Route:** Student visa subclass 500

1. Receive Confirmation of Enrolment from approved provider
2. Prepare Genuine Student evidence, finances, OSHC, English and academic documents
3. Apply online
4. Pay visa application charge
5. Complete biometrics/health checks if requested
6. Wait for decision

## Fees

- Student visa application: AUD 2,500 (from 1 July 2026; concessions apply in limited circumstances)

## Work Rights

Generally up to 48 hours a fortnight while course is in session; exceptions apply.

## Important Notes

- Visa charge increased from 1 July 2026.
- Use the official pricing estimator for exact cost.
- Apply after CoE issue and before course start. Processing times vary; use official processing time tools.

# MBBS Abroad

## Overview

- **Scope:** Distinct medical-study pathway separate from ANU's nine-country general study-abroad list.
- **Common destinations:** Russia, Philippines, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, Armenia, Nepal, Bangladesh

## Eligibility

- NEET-qualified Indian medical aspirants
- Students seeking lower-cost MBBS options
- Students comparing NMC-compliant foreign medical programs

## Core Counselling Points

- Verify university recognition, medium of instruction, clinical exposure, internship rules and licensing pathway.
- For Indian students, check current NMC/FMGL regulations before finalizing admission.
- Confirm total cost including tuition, hostel, food, insurance, visa, travel, local registration and exam costs.
- Do not promise medical licensure outcomes; licensing depends on current regulations and student performance.

# Dubai / United Arab Emirates

**Positioning:** Regional study destination for business, technology, hospitality, medical and transnational university campuses.

## Recommended Tests

- IELTS Academic
- TOEFL
- PTE Academic where accepted
- Duolingo where accepted by institution

## Visa Information

- **Route:** UAE student residence visa sponsored by an accredited university, educational institution, or eligible parent sponsor
- **Fee:** Varies by university, emirate, insurance, medical test, Emirates ID and deposits (exact amount not fixed in source material)

### Process

1. Secure admission from accredited UAE institution
2. University or eligible parent acts as sponsor/guarantor
3. Submit passport, photos, admission/enrolment evidence and other required forms
4. Complete medical fitness and Emirates ID steps where required
5. Receive residence permit

### Deadlines

Follow the university's admission and visa processing timeline. Start immediately after admission for international students needing sponsorship.

### Notes

- Student residence is typically tied to continuing enrolment.
- Outstanding students may qualify for UAE Golden Visa routes.

# France

## Overview

- **Positioning:** Destination for bachelor's, master's, business, hospitality, arts, language and Francophone pathways.

## Eligibility

Requires admission. Confirm whether Etudes en France (EEF) procedure applies based on country of application.

## Recommended Tests

- IELTS Academic
- TOEFL
- French DELF/TCF/TEF depending on program
- Duolingo where accepted

## Visa Process

**Route:** Long-stay student visa / VLS-TS student

1. Confirm whether Etudes en France procedure applies
2. Secure admission
3. Complete Campus France/EEF steps where required
4. Complete France-Visas application
5. Prepare documents and proof of resources
6. Attend appointment
7. Validate residence formalities after arrival if required

## Fees

- Student visa (EEF procedure countries): EUR 50
- Student visa (other countries): EUR 99
- Fees are indicative and may vary by special case or service provider charge

## Important Notes

- Apply after admission and ahead of intake.
- For applications submitted from 1 August 2026, minimum resources are EUR 877.50 per month.

# Visa Concepts Glossary

Reusable visa and study-abroad definitions for counselling context.

## SELT

**Secure English Language Test.** Some governments require approved English tests for specific visa routes. Acceptance depends on country, visa category, provider, and test delivery mode.

## Statement of Purpose (SOP)

A written explanation of the applicant's study goal, course choice, career plan, funding, and intention to comply with visa rules.

## Genuine Student / Genuine Temporary Entrant Assessment

A visa officer's assessment of whether the applicant genuinely intends to study and has credible academic, financial, and career reasons.

## Proof of Funds

Evidence that the applicant can pay tuition, living expenses, travel, insurance, and other required costs. Accepted evidence and minimum amounts vary by country.

## Admission Confirmation Documents

Country-specific enrolment evidence such as Australia's CoE, UK's CAS, USA Form I-20, or a university offer/admission letter.

## Biometrics

Fingerprint and photo collection required by many visa systems. It is usually completed after online application submission or during an appointment.

## Visa Fee Volatility

Visa fees, health surcharges, biometrics, service charges, and exchange-rate conversions change. Always verify against official government or visa-application-centre pages before payment.
